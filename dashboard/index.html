How the radar scores

The snapshot lists 758 protocols with a TVL (DefiLlama, 2026-11-14). 128 of them already have a token, and they teach the model. The other 342 have no token yet — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol — and those are the launches to watch.

The model is one comps curve, fitted live from the snapshot: mcap = a · TVL^b with a ≈ 1058, b ≈ 0.522, fitted by log-log OLS on the 128 priced protocols and refit every time the page builds. It is not a flat multiple: the mcap/TVL ratio collapses as protocols get bigger (0.65 below $10M TVL, 0.01 above $10B), so a flat multiple overvalues the giants and undervalues the small.

Bootstrap stability (200 seeded refits): the curve’s slope sits at 0.378–0.672 with 90% confidence, so the curve is not one lucky fit. The same 200 refits give every protocol a value band — low / central / high — and the verdict is taken at the low end: a plan must pay for itself even if the launch comes in at the bottom of the band. The band is the fit’s own uncertainty; the full prediction error is wider (leave-one-out median 3.4×), and the dashboard does not hide that.

Growth, age and per-category multipliers were tried and rejected — 21 variants in all (growth and age terms, per-category offsets, robust/Huber and winsorised fits, a cleaner training set, shrunk tilts, k-NN local regression, a broken-stick fit, a quadratic log-ratio, L1 median regression). None beat the curve on leave-one-out or on both time splits. Growth is negatively correlated with the multiple (fast growers are already priced in) and age carries no signal at all (correlation −0.02). The curve is the whole model because the data says so — the full numbers are reproducible from the repo’s scripts.

Coverage. The board ranks 202 protocols: the full 40-protocol house watchlist plus every other pre-token protocol that is a genuine launch candidate. Excluded, with reasons: centralised exchanges, liquid-staking / staking / bridge families (their token is a receipt or a wrapped asset), versioned deployments, wrapped assets. One watchlist member (Reti Pooling, a staking pool) enters through the watchlist, not the filter.

Farm verdict — costed to the dollar. Each row carries a concrete plan: capital at risk by TVL tier ($50–$250), gas priced per transaction on the protocol’s own chain (estimates, Nov 2026: Ethereum $12.00, BSC $0.15, Arbitrum/Optimism $0.30, Base $0.10, Polygon $0.05, Solana $0.01, anything else $1.00 — 2 setup transactions + one weekly action + 2 claim transactions), and a hold window (30–60 days, longer for younger protocols). The verdict comes from the low-end cover — the band’s low value divided by the plan’s all-in cost: FARM at 100,000× and above, THIN at 50,000–100,000×, SKIP below 50,000×. Thresholds sit on the tertiles of this board’s own low-end cover distribution (median ≈ 73,000×), so the split is 70 FARM / 69 THIN / 63 SKIP. Every plan also states its break-even — the share of the token supply your airdrop must be to cover the all-in cost (board median 0.0014%).

Snapshot: DefiLlama protocols, 2026-11-14. No network calls, no tracking, no prices fetched live — every number on this page comes from that one snapshot and the curve fitted to it. Gas prices are estimates; re-price any plan by multiplying its transaction count by the chain’s current per-transaction fee.
