TOKEN LAUNCH RADAR

Every pre-token protocol in the snapshot, scored by what its token would be worth — and a farm plan for each one.

How the radar scores

The snapshot lists 758 protocols with a TVL (DefiLlama, 2026-11-14). 128 of them already have a token, and they teach the model. The other 342 have no token yet — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol — and those are the launches to watch.

The model is one comps curve, fitted live from the snapshot: mcap = a · TVL^b with a ≈ 1058, b ≈ 0.522, fitted by log-log OLS on the 128 priced protocols and refit every time the page builds. It is not a flat multiple: the mcap/TVL ratio collapses as protocols get bigger (0.65 below $10M TVL, 0.01 above $10B), so a flat multiple overvalues the giants and undervalues the small. Growth, age and per-category multipliers were tried and rejected: every one scored worse out-of-sample (growth is negatively correlated with the multiple — fast growers are already priced in). The curve is the whole model.

Coverage. The board ranks 202 protocols: the full 40-protocol house watchlist plus every other pre-token protocol that is a genuine launch candidate. Excluded, with reasons: centralised exchanges, liquid-staking / staking / bridge families (their token is a receipt or a wrapped asset), versioned deployments, wrapped assets. One watchlist member (Reti Pooling, a staking pool) enters through the watchlist, not the filter.

Farm verdict. Each row carries a concrete plan: capital at risk by TVL tier ($50–$250), gas (setup + one weekly action + claim), and a hold window (30–60 days, longer for younger protocols). The verdict comes from the cover — implied value divided by the plan's all-in cost: FARM at 150,000× and above, THIN at 75,000–150,000×, SKIP below 75,000×. Thresholds sit on the tertiles of this board's own cover distribution (median ≈ 109,000×), so the split is 64 FARM / 84 THIN / 54 SKIP.
